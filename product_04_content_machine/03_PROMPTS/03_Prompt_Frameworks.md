# Prompt Frameworks for Content Generation
## 12 Copy-Paste AI Prompt Templates for Hooks, Posts, Threads, and Video Scripts

**Instructions:** Paste these into ChatGPT or Claude. Replace all [BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS] with your specifics. Each prompt is engineered to output platform-ready content with no editing needed.

## SECTION 1: HOOK GENERATOR PROMPTS (H1–H4)

### H1 — Topic Hook Generator
**Use when:** You have a theme and need scroll-stopping hooks
**Output:** 10 hooks in 60 seconds

**Prompt:**
"Generate 10 hook variations for content about [YOUR THEME] targeting [YOUR AUDIENCE, E.G., "freelance designers"]. I post on [PLATFORM, E.G., "LinkedIn"].

For each hook:
- 5–12 words
- Makes someone stop scrolling
- Works as a standalone post opener OR video hook
- Avoids generic phrases like "Here's what nobody tells you" or "The truth about"

Categorize each as:
- [A] Pattern interrupt (challenges a belief)
- [B] Curiosity gap (leaves something unfinished)
- [C] Identity statement (says something your audience believes about themselves)
- [D] Stat or specificity hook (uses a number or unexpected detail)
- [E] Outcome hook (shows a desirable result)

Return as a numbered list with category label."

---

### H2 — Angle Hook Variations
**Use when:** You have a hook you like and want to test 3 angles
**Output:** 3 variations of the same hook from different emotional angles

**Prompt:**
"I have this hook: [PASTE HOOK]. Write 3 variations using these specific angles:
1. Fear-based: What is the worst thing that happens if they ignore this?
2. Aspiration-based: What does life look like AFTER they get this?
3.叛逆 (Anti-establishment): What common advice or belief does this hook challenge?

Each variation should be 8–15 words and maintain the core message of the original hook."

---

### H3 — Question Hook Library
**Use when:** You want hooks that create engagement through questions
**Output:** 8 question-based hooks

**Prompt:**
"Generate 8 question hooks for [TOPIC]. Each should be a real question your [AUDIENCE] would actually want to answer. Format:

[QUESTION] — [What type of question it is: Rhetorical / Personal Reflection / Provocative / Challenge]

Rules:
- No yes/no questions
- Each question should make the reader feel something specific (curious, defensive, seen, challenged)
- At least 3 should be ones most people in this space are afraid to ask

Return as a numbered list."

---

### H4 — Carousel Title Hook Generator
**Use when:** Creating LinkedIn or Instagram carousel content
**Output:** 6 carousel title hooks

**Prompt:**
"Generate 6 carousel title hooks for a [NUMBER, E.G., "7-slide"] carousel about [TOPIC] for [PLATFORM]. Each title should:
- Be 4–8 words
- Create curiosity or promise a specific takeaway
- Make someone think 'I need to see what this says'
- NOT use the word 'things' or 'ways' (overused openers)

Give 2 options per category:
- Category 1: Number-led ("The 5 X That [Verb]")
- Category 2: Outcome-led ("How to [Desired Outcome] Without [Pain]")
- Category 3: Challenge-led ("Stop [Common Mistake]. Do This Instead.")

## SECTION 2: POST EXPANDER PROMPTS (P1–P3)

### P1 — Short Post Expander (150–200 words)
**Use when:** You have a hook and need a full platform-ready post
**Output:** Complete post with hook, body, CTA, hashtags

**Prompt:**
"Write a [PLATFORM, E.G., "LinkedIn"] post of exactly 150–200 words about [YOUR HOOK OR TOPIC].

Structure:
- Opening line (hook): [Must make someone stop scrolling — write it as the first line, not a headline]
- Paragraph 1 (48–60 words): Set up the problem or observation — be specific, use a real scenario or data point
- Paragraph 2 (48–60 words): Give the insight, lesson, or main point — make it specific and actionable
- Paragraph 3 (30–40 words): End with a challenge or question that drives comments
- Call to action (10–15 words): Ask a question OR invite them to save/share/follow
- Hashtags: 3–5 specific hashtags (not generic ones like #motivation or #success)

Tone: [DIRECT / CONVERSATIONAL / WITTY / EMOTIONAL — choose one]
Audience: [AUDIENCE DESCRIPTION]

Write it as if a real human wrote it. No listicles, no filler phrases."

---

### P2 — Long-Form Post Generator (300–500 words)
**Use when:** You want a thought leadership piece for LinkedIn or a newsletter
**Output:** Full post with structure, depth, and engagement hooks

**Prompt:**
"Write a [300–500 word] [PLATFORM] post on [TOPIC OR HOOK].

Format:
1. Hook line (first line must stop the scroll — make it surprising, specific, or emotionally charged)
2. Why this matters (1 paragraph — who this is for and why you're writing about this now)
3. The main content (3–4 paragraphs — each starts with a bold statement or insight, then 2–3 sentences of supporting content)
4. Key takeaway (1 paragraph — the one thing the reader should do or think differently)
5. Engagement question (1 sentence — something that invites personal sharing, not generic comments like 'what do you think?')

Tone: [See tone guide below — pick one]
- Personal story: Conversational, uses "I", occasional vulnerability
- Expert authority: Confident, direct, uses "you", states positions clearly
- Provocateur: Challenges norms, uses contrast, creates debate
- Teacher: Clear, structured, explains the "why" behind the "what"

Audience level: [BEGINNER / MIXED / ADVANCED — pick one]
No fluff. No generic inspirational close. End with something the reader can actually do."

---

### P3 — Newsletter Issue Generator
**Use when:** You want a weekly newsletter edition tied to your weekly theme
**Output:** Complete email newsletter with subject line, preview text, and body

**Prompt:**
"Write a weekly newsletter issue for [AUDIENCE] on this week's theme: [THEME].

Include:
- Subject line (40 characters or under, curiosity-driven)
- Preview text (90 characters or under, one sentence that makes them open it)
- Opening paragraph (who this is for and why this week matters)
- 3 sections (each with a subheading and 2–3 paragraphs — one insight or story per section)
- Closing paragraph (recap the main point + invitation to reply)
- Sign-off (keep it personal, not corporate)

Tone: Warm, direct, like you're writing to a smart friend who shares your professional interests. Not salesy. Not formal.

Frequency context: This is a [WEEKLY/BI-WEEKLY] newsletter. The reader opted in for [WHAT VALUE THEY EXPECT]."

## SECTION 3: THREAD GENERATOR PROMPTS (T1–T2)

### T1 — Standard Thread Generator (5–7 posts)
**Use when:** You have a strong hook and want to expand it into a thread
**Output:** Complete 5–7 post thread, each post formatted separately

**Prompt:**
"Write a [5–7 post] Twitter/X thread on [TOPIC OR HOOK].

Format: Each post numbered [1/7], [2/7], etc. Each post 100–200 characters.

Structure:
- Post 1 (Hook): The single most surprising or contrarian statement about this topic — must make someone want to read the next one
- Posts 2–5 (Body): Each covers one sub-point. Each ends with a hook for the next post (a question, a cliffhanger, or a "here's what happened next" lead-in)
- Post 6 (Climax): The main insight, the turn, or the key lesson — the one thing that makes the whole thread worth reading
- Post 7 (CTA): Call to action that drives meaningful engagement — ask them something, don't just say "follow for more"

Rules:
- Every post must work standalone — someone who only reads one post should still get something
- Use specific numbers, names, or stories — not "research shows" without specifics
- Write like a person, not a brand

Topic: [YOUR TOPIC]
Angle: [WHAT SPECIFIC PERSPECTIVE OR CONCLUSION YOU WANT TO ARRIVE AT]"

---

### T2 — Hot Take Thread Generator
**Use when:** You want to post a strong opinion that generates debate
**Output:** 5-post contrarian thread

**Prompt:**
"Write a 5-post hot take thread on [TOPIC]. The thread should argue the OPPOSITE of conventional wisdom in your space — something that makes people feel seen and disagree with at the same time.

Format: [1/5] through [5/5], each post 100–200 characters.

Structure:
- [1/5]: The contrarian claim — state it directly, make it sound extreme
- [2/5]: The evidence or argument — real data, a story, or a pattern you've noticed
- [3/5]: The acknowledgment (give an inch) — "Here's what I'll admit is true about the other side..."
- [4/5]: The pivot — "But here's what nobody talks about..."
- [5/5]: The close — a challenge to the reader + invitation to argue back

Tone: Confident, slightly provocative, intellectually honest. Not mean. Not dismissive.

Topic: [YOUR TOPIC]"

## SECTION 4: VIDEO SCRIPT PROMPTS (V1–V2)

### V1 — Short Video Script Generator (30–60 seconds)
**Use when:** Creating TikTok, Reels, or YouTube Shorts scripts
**Output:** Complete spoken script with on-screen text cues

**Prompt:**
"Write a [30 / 60 / 90]-second video script for [PLATFORM] about [TOPIC].

Format:
- [ON SCREEN]: What appears as text on screen at this moment
- [NARRATOR]: What you say, written exactly as you would say it aloud

Structure:
- 0–3 seconds: Hook — a visual or statement that stops the scroll
- 3–20 seconds: Problem — the wrong approach or common mistake
- 20–45 seconds: Solution — the insight, framed practically
- 45–[60/90] seconds: CTA — follow, save, or link in bio

Specs:
- Speak at pace of approximately 150 words per minute for [PLATFORM, E.G., "TikTok"]
- No umms, uhs, or filler phrases
- The on-screen text should complement what you're saying, not repeat it word-for-word
- End with a question or challenge that drives comments

Topic: [WHAT THE VIDEO IS ABOUT]
Key message you want them to remember: [ONE SENTENCE]"

---

### V2 — Hook-to-Video Outline Generator
**Use when:** You have a strong post or thread and want to turn it into a video
**Output:** Talking points outline with on-screen graphic suggestions

**Prompt:**
"Take this piece of content: [PASTE YOUR POST, HOOK, OR THREAD HERE] and turn it into a [30 / 60 / 90]-second video script for [PLATFORM].

For each section of your content, specify:
- [OPENING GRAPHIC]: What should appear on screen (image, text overlay, or clip description)
- [AUDIO]: Is this voiceover, talking head, or text-to-speech?
- [KEY POINT]: The one thing you say in this segment
- [VISUAL CUE]: A specific visual or cut that illustrates the point

Also suggest:
- 3 hashtags (use # to start each)
- 1 sound or music trend on [PLATFORM] that would work well with this type of content
- Best time to post based on [PLATFORM]'s algorithm (general guidance)

Format your output as a simple video production brief."

## SECTION 5: ENGAGEMENT CAPTION PROMPTS (E1)

### E1 — Caption Variation Generator
**Use when:** You have an image or concept and need 3 caption options
**Output:** 3 complete captions in different styles

**Prompt:**
"Write 3 different Instagram or [PLATFORM] captions for this post about [TOPIC].

Caption 1 — The Question Opener: Starts with a question that stops the scroll and gets people commenting
Caption 2 — The Bold Statement: A strong opinion or counterintuitive take that makes people either love it or argue back
Caption 3 — The Vulnerability Play: A personal or self-deprecating opening that builds connection

For each caption:
- Opening line (most important — write 3 variations)
- Body (2–4 sentences, optional depending on format)
- CTA (what you want people to do after reading: comment, save, share, or click link)
- Hashtags (3–5, place after the caption, not in the caption itself)

Platform: [PLATFORM]
Audience: [AUDIENCE DESCRIPTION]
Post topic: [TOPIC]"
