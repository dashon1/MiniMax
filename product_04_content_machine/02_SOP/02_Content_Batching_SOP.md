# Content Batching SOP
## The Step-by-Step System for Your Weekly 15–30 Minute Content Session

**Estimated time: 15–30 minutes | Frequency: Once per week**

---

## Before You Start

**Tools you need (all free):**
- ChatGPT or Claude (free tier is fine)
- `03_Prompt_Frameworks.md` from this kit — open in a second tab
- `04_Weekly_Planner_Template.md` — open or print
- A scheduling tool (Buffer free tier, Hypefury, Later, or even a Google Sheet)

**Environment setup (2 minutes):**
1. Close Slack, email, and all other tabs
2. Put your phone on Do Not Disturb
3. Make a cup of coffee or tea
4. Set a timer for 30 minutes — this keeps you moving

## PHASE 1 — DEFINE YOUR WEEKLY THEME (5 minutes)

**The one question to answer:**
*"What is the ONE thing my audience needs to understand, learn, or do this week?"*

## PHASE 2 — GENERATE YOUR HOOKS (5–7 minutes)

**Open:** `03_Prompt_Frameworks.md` → Section 1: Hook Generator Prompts

**Run Prompt H1 (Topic Hooks):**
Paste into ChatGPT: *"Generate 10 hooks for a week of content about [YOUR THEME]. Each hook should be 5–12 words and make someone want to stop scrolling. Write for [YOUR PLATFORM]."*

**Output:** 10 hook options. Read them out loud. Mark your top 3.

**Run Prompt H2 (Angle Hooks) — optional but recommended:**
*"For each of my top 3 hooks, generate 2 variations using different angles: one fear-based, one aspiration-based."*

## PHASE 3 — EXPAND HOOKS INTO CONTENT (10–15 minutes)

**For each hook, pick a format and expand:**

**Option A: Short Post (150–200 words)**
Run Prompt P1 (Short Post Expander) for Hook 1 and Hook 2.
*"Write a [150–200 word] post for [PLATFORM] on the topic of [HOOK]. Include: one hook line at the top, 3 short paragraphs, a call to action at the end, and 3 relevant hashtags."*

**Option B: Thread (5–7 tweets/posts)**
Run Prompt T1 (Thread Generator) for Hook 3 or your strongest hook.
*"Write a [5–7 post] thread for [PLATFORM] on [HOOK]. Each post should be self-contained, end with a cliffhanger or hook for the next one, and end with a strong call to action."*

**Option C: Video or Carousel Script (30–90 second script)**
Run Prompt V1 (Video Script Generator) for any hook you want to turn into visual content.
*"Write a [60-second] video script for [PLATFORM] on [HOOK]. Format: [Hook line on screen / narrator says]. [Body — 3–4 key points]. [CTA: Follow for more like this / Link in bio]."*

## PHASE 5 — REPURPOSE AND CROSS-POST (ongoing)

**Open:** `05_Repurposing_Checklist.md`

**The repurposing rule:** Every piece you create should be adapted for at least 2 other platforms before you schedule it.

For each piece in your planner, decide:
1. **Original format:** Where does this live first?
2. **Adapt 1:** Turn it into a different format for another platform
3. **Adapt 2:** Turn it into a third format or angle

*Example: A LinkedIn post → becomes → a Twitter/X thread → becomes → an Instagram carousel caption.*

---

## POST-SESSION: SCHEDULE AND PUBLISH

**For each piece in your planner:**
1. Paste into your scheduling tool
2. Add platform-specific hashtags
3. Set the day and time
4. Confirm and repeat

**After scheduling:**
- Set a recurring calendar reminder for the same time next week
- Before your next session, review which posts performed best — feed this back into your hook selection

| Phase | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Define weekly theme | 5 min | 1 theme, 1 primary platform |
| 2. Generate hooks | 5 min | 10 hooks, keep 3 |
| 3. Expand to content | 15 min | 3–5 pieces ready to post |
| 4. Fill planner | 5 min | 7-day content calendar filled |
| **Total** | **30 min** | **1 week of content, done** |

---

## WHAT TO DO BETWEEN SESSIONS

- Engage with comments on your scheduled posts
- Watch how your audience responds to different formats
- Note any patterns that emerge — save them for next week's theme
- Do NOT create new content between sessions (this defeats the system)
