## Turn 1 Piece of Content Into 5 — Without Creating More From Scratch

**Core principle:** Every piece you create should be adapted across at least 2 additional platforms before you schedule it. The repurposing happens in the last 10 minutes of your batch session, not as a separate task.

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC ADAPTATION NOTES

### Twitter/X
- Long posts → cut to 280 chars or turn into 3-post thread
- Keep the most surprising 20% as the hook
- Add 1 hashtag (use # format, max 1)
- Change formal tone to conversational

### LinkedIn
- Threads → single long-form post (150–300 words)
- Add a professional framing line at the top
- End with a question that drives comments from peers
- Use 3–5 hashtags, mix of broad + niche

### Instagram
- Carousel → caption that stands alone without the slides
- Start with a hook line (before the carousel slides show)
- Use line breaks to create white space
- Add 5–8 hashtags (mix: 3 niche, 2 broad, 2 community)

### Facebook
- Long posts → cut to 100–150 words
- Remove all hashtags
- Change professional tone to conversational
- Add a personal question at the end

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- Blog post or newsletter → convert to "things I wish I knew about X" format
- Start with the most counter-intuitive point
- Use a talking-head format OR screen-record while narrating
- CTA: Follow for more, link in bio

### Newsletter
- Any social post → expand into a full newsletter edition
- Add a "why I'm writing about this now" opening
- Include a personal angle or behind-the-scenes note
- End with an open question and invitation to reply

## THE 10-MINUTE END-OF-SESSION REPURPOSE ROUTINE

*(Do this at the end of every batch session before you close the tab)*

**Minute 1–2:** Open all the posts you created today in separate tabs
**Minute 3–4:** Pick the best 2–3 pieces to repurpose
**Minute 5–6:** For each, note which platform it should also go on
**Minute 7–9:** Make the 2–3 adaptations (short versions, thread cuts, or carousel notes)
**Minute 10:** Add repurposed content to your planner for tomorrow OR schedule immediately

---

## WHAT TO REPURPOSE AND WHAT TO LEAVE ALONE

**Always repurpose:**
- Posts with a strong opinion or hot take
- Posts that got good engagement (comments = repurposing gold)
- Educational content (it works across every format)
- "Things I wish I knew about X" posts (perfect for carousel → video → thread)

**Leave as-is (don't repurpose):**
- Very time-sensitive news or commentary
- Personal moments or life updates (adapt tone, don't force onto every platform)
- Jokes or memes (platform-native, doesn't translate well)
- Engagement bait ("Comment X if you...") — doesn't work in professional formats

## SIGNALS THAT A PIECE IS WORTH REPURPOSING HEAVILY

Mark these during your batch session and prioritize them:

- [ ] The hook made you slightly uncomfortable (strong opinion = high repurposing value)
- [ ] The insight is counterintuitive (people want to share things that make them look smart)
- [ ] You learned something while writing it (personal discovery angle = newsletter gold)
- [ ] It's a process or framework (step-by-step = carousel format, then thread, then video)
- [ ] It could start with "The problem with..." (provocative opener = high hook value)
