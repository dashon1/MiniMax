## The Core Idea: Batch, Don't Drip

Most creators produce content one piece at a time — and burn out. The alternative is batch mode:

1. **Once a week** (pick a day, protect it): Open your prompt frameworks, open ChatGPT, and generate 7–14 pieces of content in one session.
2. **Schedule them** using a free tool like Buffer, Hypefury, or Later.
3. **Repurpose** each piece across platforms instead of creating from scratch for each one.

This is how creators who post consistently actually work. It is not talent. It is system.

## The 4-Phase Weekly Workflow

### Phase 1: Choose Your Direction (5 minutes)
Open your Weekly Planner. Pick your ONE core theme for the week. Everything you post this week connects to that theme. This is what makes your content feel like a conversation instead of random noise.

Ask yourself: *What is the one thing my audience needs to hear or learn this week?*

### Phase 2: Generate Your Hooks (5 minutes)
Open `03_PROMPTS`. Run Hook Generator Prompt 1 (Topic Hooks) for your week's theme. Get 10 hooks. Keep 3. These are your content anchors for the week.

### Phase 3: Expand Into Posts (10–15 minutes)
For each hook you kept, run the Post Expander Prompt. Each hook becomes a full post, thread, or video script. Use the Platform Adapter section to customize tone and length per platform.

### Phase 4: Fill Your Calendar (5 minutes)
Open `04_PLANNER`. Assign each piece to a specific day and platform. Add your repurposing assignments. You now have a complete, ready-to-schedule content week.

## The 5-Platform Cheat Sheet

| Platform | Ideal Length | Best Format | Posting Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Twitter/X** | 100–280 chars | Single tweet or 3–5 thread | Daily or 2x/day |
| **LinkedIn** | 150–300 words | Carousel or long-form post | 3–5x/week |
| **Instagram** | Caption 150–200 words + hook line | Carousel or single image | 4–6x/week |
| **Facebook** | 100–200 words | Short post or video caption | 1–2x/day |
| **TikTok** | 1-sentence hook + body | Video script 30–90 seconds | Daily |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| No theme for the week | Each week picks ONE topic. Content without a theme looks random and unfollowable. |
| Posting the same format every day | Mix it up: post, then thread, then carousel, then short video script. |
| Creating for every platform from scratch | Repurpose FIRST. Create once, adapt 3–5 times. |
| Not batch scheduling | Use a scheduler. Post nothing manually. The goal is to spend 15 minutes and be done for the week. |
| No hook framework | Always start with hooks. Hook gets the attention. Everything else is secondary. |
