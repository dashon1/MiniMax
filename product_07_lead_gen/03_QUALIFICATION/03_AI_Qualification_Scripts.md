# AI Qualification Scripts
## 8 Copy-Paste Prompt Templates for Qualifying, Scoring, and Routing Leads Automatically

**How to use:** Paste these into ChatGPT or Claude as part of your email sequence. Each script is designed to work in a conversational email format — not a chatbot. Leads reply naturally, AI analyzes the responses, and you get a scored lead waiting in your CRM.

---

## SCRIPT Q1 — WELCOME + FIRST QUALIFYING QUESTION
**Purpose:** Welcome the lead and ask the first qualifying question
**Send timing:** Day 1 (immediately after form submission)

---

**Subject:** [First Name], let me ask you a few questions first

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for signing up for [Lead Magnet Name]. I've got it ready for you — check your inbox for the download link.

Before you go, I'd like to understand a bit more about your situation so I can make sure what I've put together is actually useful for you.

Here's my first question:

How are you currently handling [the problem your product solves]? Are you doing it manually, using a tool or service, or not doing it at all?

Just reply to this email — I'll read every response personally.

[Your Name]
```

**AI Analysis Prompt (to process replies):**

```
Analyze this lead response and score it:
"[LEAD RESPONSE]"

Score on a scale of 1–5 for the following:
1. Budget awareness (1=no budget mentioned, 5=mentions specific budget)
2. Current solution (1=does nothing, 5=already paying for a solution)
3. Pain clarity (1=vague, 5=specific problem described)

Also identify: Are they actively solving this problem or just exploring?
```

## SCRIPT Q2 — TIMELINE AND AUTHORITY QUESTIONS
**Purpose:** Ask about timing and decision-making authority
**Send timing:** Day 2 (24 hours after Q1 response)

---

**Subject:** Quick follow-up, [First Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for that response — it really helps me understand where you're at.

Two quick questions:

1. **Timing:** When are you looking to have [the outcome] sorted out? Are you looking to get this handled within the next month, or is this a longer-term project?

2. **Authority:** Are you the person making the final decision on this, or is there someone else involved?

Just hit reply — takes 30 seconds.

[Your Name]
```

**AI Analysis Prompt:**

```
Analyze this lead response and score it:
"[BOTH ANSWERS FROM LEAD]"

Score:
1. Timeline (1=exploring/no timeline, 5=immediate/within 1 month)
2. Authority (1=not decision-maker, 5=sole decision-maker)

Also flag: Is this lead likely to close within 30 days?
```

## SCRIPT Q3 — PROBLEM DEPTH AND URGENCY
**Purpose:** Measure problem urgency and emotional investment
**Send timing:** Day 3 (24 hours after Q2 response)

---

**Subject:** One more question, then I'll send your [Lead Magnet]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Last question, I promise — then I'll send over [Lead Magnet Name] right away.

On a scale of 1 to 10, how much is [the problem] getting in the way of [their desired outcome] right now?

A 1 means it's just a nice-to-have someday. A 10 means it's burning a hole in your desk and you need it sorted before the month is out.

No wrong answer — I ask everyone this.

[Your Name]
```

**AI Analysis Prompt:**

```
Score this lead urgency response:
"[LEAD RESPONSE]"

Score urgency: 1=exploring someday, 5=moderate pain, 10=critical/urgent

Also note: What emotional signals are present? (frustration, excitement, resignation, etc.)
What does this tell us about their likelihood to buy in the next 30 days?
```

## SCRIPT Q4 — HOT LEAD CONFIRMATION + BOOKING TRIGGER
**Purpose:** Sent when lead scores 21+ (Hot). Converts interest into a meeting.
**Send timing:** Day 4 (24 hours after Q3 score calculated)

---

**Subject:** [First Name] — I think we should talk

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Based on what you've shared, I think a short conversation could be really valuable for you.

Here's what I'd like to offer: A free [30–45 minute] discovery call. No pitch, no obligation. Just a conversation where I ask you some questions about where you're at, and you decide if what I do makes sense for where you want to go.

On the call, I'll have specific questions prepared — things that will help me understand whether I can actually help you get to [their stated outcome].

If you're interested, here is my calendar:
[YOUR CALENDLY / CAL.COM LINK]

Pick any slot that works. If none of those work, reply and I'll find a time.

If you're not ready for a call yet, no pressure at all — I'll send you the [Lead Magnet] now and you can reach out when the timing is right.

[Your Name]
```

## SCRIPT Q5 — WARM LEAD NURTURE BRIDGE
**Purpose:** Sent when lead scores 14–20 (Warm). Builds urgency and offers booking.
**Send timing:** Day 4

---

**Subject:** While it's on my mind, [First Name]...

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to reach out while this is fresh.

You've clearly put some thought into [the problem] — and from what you've shared, you're in a position where getting the right solution could make a real difference in the next few months.

Most people I work with who are in a similar situation tell me two things:
1. They wish they'd made the decision sooner
2. They didn't know how to evaluate options properly

I've put together a [Free Audit / Quick Evaluation] that takes about 20 minutes and gives you a clear picture of where you're at and what the best path forward looks like. Happy to do that with you no charge if it would be useful.

If not, totally fine — I'll keep you on my list for when you're ready.

Reply and let me know what makes sense.

[Your Name]
```

```
Here's what to expect over the next few weeks:

I'll send you a few emails with thoughts on [the problem space]. Some of it will be directly related to your situation. Some might be tangential. All of it, I hope, useful.

If you ever decide you want to chat, my calendar is always here:
[YOUR CALENDLY LINK]

No pressure. No sales pitch. Just a conversation if it makes sense.

Talk soon,

[Your Name]
```

## SCRIPT Q7 — POST-CALL AUTOMATIC FOLLOW-UP
**Purpose:** Sent automatically after a discovery call to gather feedback and advance the relationship.
**Send timing:** 2 hours after call ends

---

**Subject:** Notes from our call, [First Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

Great talking with you today. A few things I want to confirm:

[SHORT SUMMARY OF WHAT WAS DISCUSSED — 2–3 sentences]

Here are the next steps we agreed on:
1. [Next step 1 — who does what]
2. [Next step 2]
3. [Next step 3]

If I missed anything or if anything changed in the last few hours, just reply and let me know.

I'm going to send over a [proposal / follow-up document / pricing] by [date]. Keep an eye out for it.

Talk soon,

[Your Name]
```

## SCRIPT Q8 — RE-ENGAGEMENT FOR DORMANT LEADS
**Purpose:** Re-activates leads who went cold (opened 5+ nurture emails but never converted)
**Send timing:** Day 45 after last activity

---

**Subject:** Is [the problem] still getting in the way?

**Body:**

```
Hi [Name],

I've been following your story for a while now, and I know you've probably gotten a few emails from me by now.

I'm going to be direct: Are you still dealing with [the problem]?

If the answer is yes — I'd love to know what's stopped you from making a move. Is it timing, budget, something else?

Reply and let me know what's going on. Even just "still interested, just not ready yet" helps me know how to stay in touch in a way that's useful for you.

If the answer is no — you've solved it another way — that's great too. Just let me know and I'll update my notes so I stop bothering you.

Either way, I appreciate you being on the list.

[Your Name]
```
