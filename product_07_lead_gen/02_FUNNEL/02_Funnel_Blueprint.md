## STAGE 1 — CAPTURE

**What it is:** A landing page with a lead magnet offer. Visitor exchanges their email for something valuable.

### Lead Magnet Options by Business Type

| Business Type | Lead Magnet | Capture Page Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Coach / Consultant | Free discovery call | "Book a free 30-min strategy call" |
| Freelancer / Agency | Portfolio or case study | "See how I got [result] for [client type]" |
| Course creator | Free module or cheat sheet | "Get [specific outcome] with this free guide" |
| SaaS / Tool | Free trial or calculator | "Calculate your [metric] in 60 seconds" |
| E-commerce | Discount or free shipping | "Get 15% off your first order" |

### Capture Page Requirements
- Headline: ONE clear promise (8–12 words)
- Subheadline: What it is + who it's for (1 sentence)
- Bullets: 3 specific outcomes (not features)
- Form: Name + Email (2 fields only — never more on the capture stage)
- CTA button: Action verb ("Get My Free Guide" / "Book My Free Call")
- Trust signal: "Join X people who have..." or a brief guarantee line

### Post-Capture Automation
- Trigger: Form submitted
- Action 1: CRM entry created (Status: New Lead, Score: 0, Source: [captured])
- Action 2: Welcome email sent (immediate, within 2 minutes)
- Action 3: Qualification sequence triggered (Day 1)

## STAGE 2 — QUALIFICATION

**What it is:** An AI-powered 3–5 question sequence that segments leads by readiness and fit.

### The Qualification Framework (5 Questions)

**Q1 — Budget qualifier**
"How much are you currently investing in [the problem your product solves]?"
- $0–$99: Low budget (score 1)
- $100–$499: Medium budget (score 3)
- $500+: High budget (score 5)
- "I'm just exploring": Exploratory (score 1)

**Q2 — Timeline qualifier**
"When are you looking to [desired outcome]?"
- Immediately (within 1 month): Hot (score 5)
- 1–3 months: Warm (score 4)
- 3–6 months: Planning (score 3)
- "Someday": Cold (score 1)

**Q3 — Authority qualifier**
"Are you the decision-maker for this purchase?"
- Yes, I decide alone: Score 5
- I influence it: Score 3
- Someone else decides: Score 1

**Q4 — Problem urgency qualifier**
"On a scale of 1–10, how painful is [the problem] right now?"
- 9–10 (critical): Hot (score 5)
- 7–8 (important): Warm (score 4)
- 4–6 (mildly aware): Cool (score 2)
- 1–3 (not urgent): Cold (score 1)

**Q5 — Fit qualifier**
"Which best describes your situation?"
- "I have tried [similar solution] before but it didn't work": Score 4 (proven buyer)
- "I'm doing this manually and it's costing me time": Score 3 (aware of pain)
- "I'm just starting to explore options": Score 2 (early stage)
- "My company doesn't have budget this quarter": Score 1 (wrong timing)

### Scoring Thresholds

| Total Score | Lead Type | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 21–25 | Hot — Book immediately | Send booking link immediately |
| 14–20 | Warm — Nurture to book | Send booking link + urgency sequence |
| 8–13 | Cool — Long nurture | Enroll in 7-email sequence |
| 0–7 | Cold — Low priority | Auto-nurture (no urgency) |

### Sequence Send Timing
- Day 1: Welcome + Q1
- Day 2: Q2 + Q3 (based on Q1 response)
- Day 3: Q4 + Q5
- Day 4: Score calculated → routing triggered

## STAGE 3 — BOOKING FLOW

**Trigger:** Lead score 14+ reached (Hot or Warm)

**Email 1 — Booking Invitation (sent within 1 hour of qualification complete)**

Subject: [First Name], let's see if we're a fit.

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for going through the qualification. Based on what you've shared, I think a conversation could be valuable.

I'd love to learn more about [specific thing they mentioned]. If you're open to it, I'd like to offer you a free [30/45/60]-minute discovery call.

The goal of the call: Find out if we're a fit for each other. I'll ask questions, you'll ask questions, and we'll both decide if it makes sense to work together.

Here's my calendar — pick a time that works:
[YOUR CALENDLY / CAL.COM LINK]

No pitch. No pressure. Just a real conversation.

[Your Name]
```

**Pre-Call Confirmation Email (sent instantly on booking):**

Subject: Your call is confirmed — [Date] at [Time] — [Your Name]

```
Hi [Name],

Your discovery call is confirmed.

📅 [Date] at [Time] ([Timezone])
🔗 [Video call link — Calendly/Zoom]
⏰ Duration: [30/45/60] minutes

Before we jump on:

A few things I'll want to know:
1. What's the current situation with [their main problem]?
2. What have you already tried to solve it?
3. What would "success" look like for you in the next 90 days?

There's nothing to prepare beyond that. Just show up and be ready to talk.

If anything changes and you need to reschedule, just use the link in your confirmation email. No charge, no questions asked.

See you on [Date].

[Your Name]
```

## STAGE 4 — NURTURE SEQUENCES

### Hot Lead Sequence (score 21+)
Days 1, 2, 3 — urgency emails + booking link repeated
Day 5 — "Did you book?" follow-up
Day 7 — "Is this still a priority?" follow-up

### Warm Lead Sequence (score 14–20)
Days 1, 4, 8, 14 — value emails + booking link
Day 21 — "Where are you at?" full re-engagement

### Cool Lead Sequence (score 8–13)
7-email sequence over 30 days
Week 1: Value emails (problem awareness)
Week 2: Case study / social proof
Week 3: Transformation stories
Week 4: Soft offer + booking link

### Cold Lead Sequence (score 0–7)
Monthly newsletter (low frequency, high value)
Auto-escalates if: opens 5 emails OR clicks any link = score +10
