## The 4-Stage Funnel

**Stage 1 — Capture**
The lead lands on your page, sees your offer, and exchanges their email for something valuable (a free audit, a mini-course, a template). Their email goes into your CRM automatically.

**Stage 2 — Qualification**
AI sends a sequence that asks 3–5 qualifying questions. Responses are scored. Hot leads are flagged. Cold leads are nurtured automatically.

**Stage 3 — Booking**
Qualified leads receive a booking link. Calendly or Cal.com. One click. Meeting on your calendar.

**Stage 4 — Confirmation**
Instant confirmation email with agenda, preparation instructions, and what to expect. You arrive at the call informed and ready.

## Setup Time: Same Day

The full system can be live by end of day:
- 2 hours: Build the funnel pages (capture page + qualification flow)
- 1 hour: Set up CRM structure and automation
- 30 minutes: Configure booking flow and test

After that: every lead that enters the funnel gets qualified and routed automatically. You check your CRM for warm leads instead of chasing cold ones.
