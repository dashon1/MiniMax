# Email Templates
## 5 Automated Emails for Your Client Onboarding Sequence

**Instructions:** Copy each template into Gmail drafts or your email marketing tool. Replace all [BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS]. Then set up the send timing in your Zapier/Make automation using the delays listed under each template.

## EMAIL 1 — Contract Confirmation (Sent immediately on signing)
**Trigger:** Contract signed (via PandaDoc, Docusign, or manual return)
**Send timing:** Immediate (Zap: contract signed → delay 0 → send email)
**Subject:** You're confirmed — here's what happens next, [CLIENT FIRST NAME]

---

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

Your contract for [PROJECT NAME] is confirmed and signed. You're officially on board — I'm glad you're here.

Here's what happens next:

**[DAY 0–1]** You'll receive an intake form within the next [X hours]. It takes about 10 minutes to fill out and helps me make sure we're set up to hit the ground running from day one.

**[DAY 1–3]** Once your intake is in, you'll receive a welcome sequence with everything you need to prepare for our kickoff.

**[KICKOFF DATE]** That's when we start. I've got [TIME] blocked on [DATE] — I'll send a calendar invite and agenda 48 hours before.

If anything comes up before then, reach me at [YOUR EMAIL]. Otherwise, I'll see you in the intake form.

Looking forward to getting started.

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR TITLE / YOUR BUSINESS NAME]

**[INTAKE FORM LINK]**

**Please complete it by [DATE — typically 48 hours from now].**

Once I have it, I'll set your kickoff call, send your logistics email, and we'll be ready to go.

If you have any questions in the meantime, reply here — I read every email.
