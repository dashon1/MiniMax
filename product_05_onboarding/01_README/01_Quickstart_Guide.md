## The 5-Phase Onboarding System

### Phase 1: The Contract & Deposit (Day 0)
**Trigger:** Client signs the contract
**Automated actions:**
- Contract returned signed (auto via PandaDoc, Docusign, or email)
- Deposit invoice sent automatically
- Payment confirmation triggers next phase
- CRM contact created automatically

### Phase 2: The Intake Form (Day 0–1)
**Trigger:** Deposit payment received
**Automated actions:**
- Intake form link sent immediately (with clear deadline)
- Reminder email sent after 24 hours if not completed
- All form responses go directly into your CRM — no manual copy-pasting

### Phase 3: The Welcome Sequence (Day 1–3)
**Trigger:** Intake form completed
**Automated email sequence kicks off:**
- Welcome email (Day 1) — excitement, what to expect, your contact info
- Logistics email (Day 2) — calendar invite, how to reach you, tool access
- Kickoff prep email (Day 3) — what to prepare for the first session/call

### Phase 4: CRM Setup + Task Creation (Day 1)
**Trigger:** Intake form completed
**Automated actions:**
- New client profile created in Notion/CRM with all intake answers
- First project created with milestones
- Team members notified (if applicable)
- Task assigned to you for kickoff preparation

### Phase 5: The Kickoff (Day 5–14, depending on your timeline)
**Trigger:** Automated from contract start date
**Automated actions:**
- Kickoff call reminder sent 48 hours before
- Kickoff agenda sent automatically
- First invoice issued (if payment-on-milestone structure)
- Post-kickoff check-in scheduled for 1 week later

## What Gets Automated

| Phase | Email Sent | CRM Updated | Task Created |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract signed | Confirmation + contract copy | Contact added, stage updated | Task: review contract |
| Deposit received | Intake form + welcome teaser | Stage: intake pending | Task: monitor intake |
| Intake complete | Welcome sequence starts | All client info populated | Project created |
| Pre-kickoff (48h) | Agenda + logistics | Stage: ready for kickoff | Task: prep for kickoff |
| Post-kickoff | Check-in + next steps | Stage: in delivery | Task: next milestone |
