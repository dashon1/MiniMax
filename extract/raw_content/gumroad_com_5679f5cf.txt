### Payout modes
We support direct bank deposits in the local currency for most countries. For countries where bank deposits are not available, we offer PayPal transfers as the only payment option.
We do \*not\* support alternative payout modes like Payoneer, Wise, check, money order, wire transfer, etc. If your country is not supported by direct bank deposits or PayPal, then unfortunately, we have no way to pay you out for now.
To get paid for your sales, start by filling out your [Payments Settings](https://gumroad.com/settings/payments).

We can only pay out to a local bank account and in your local currency. We cannot pay you out in USD if it is not listed above as your country's payout currency. All currency conversions happen based on the exchange rates at the time of sale, not at the time of the payout. These are typically mid-market rates that you can estimate [here](https://dashboard.stripe.com/currency\_conversion).
For prompt payouts, please ensure that you have the following for your country of residence:
\* A valid, government-issued photo ID
\* Proof of residence within the country
If you are selling as a business, the business must be registered in the same country. You cannot get paid to another country's bank account by just having a business registered in that country. You must physically live (and can prove residence) in that country.
Depending on your country, bank payouts can take 2-7 business days to process, and can be delayed around bank holidays.
### Get paid to PayPal
If bank payouts aren't supported in your country, we will pay you via PayPal. You just need an individual or business PayPal account without any restrictions.
All PayPal payouts are processed in USD and usually arrive within 1–3 business days.
PayPal payouts have a 2% processing fee.
### Verification process
Our payment processor, Stripe, requires additional information to verify the identity of the account holder after a certain amount of time has passed and sales accrued. This is a standard practice in the financial industry and is part of their [Know Your Customer (KYC) obligations](https://support.stripe.com/questions/know-your-customer-obligations).
### ID verification
You can learn the correct documents needed for ID verification for your specific location [here](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/handling-api-verification?country=CA&document-type=identity#acceptable-verification-documents).
### Address verification
You can learn the correct documents needed for address verification for your specific location [here](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/handling-api-verification?country=CA&document-type=address#acceptable-verification-documents).
### Social Security number (SSN)
SSN is required for US sellers. The name and date of birth on your SSN must match exactly with the information filled in your [Payments Settings](https://gumroad.com/settings/payments). Refer to your social security card to see how your name is formatted, and enter your name in the settings exactly as it is on the card. If you have a middle name, you can add it to the First Name field on the settings page.
If you don't have your Social Security card, or if you believe you've entered the correct information but still receive an error, please contact the Social Security Administration. You can request a copy of your card or update your name here: https://www.ssa.gov/ssnumber/
### Payout schedule
\*\*⚠️ You need a minimum balance of $10 USD to receive a payout.\*\* Some countries have higher local currency minimums—see the "(min X)" values in the [table above](#bank-account).
You can choose to get paid daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly, and set your own payout threshold. Daily payouts are only available for US users with eligible bank accounts and at least 4 prior payouts. You can also pause and resume payouts anytime.
Payouts include sales made through the previous Friday (UTC). All sales have a minimum 7-day holding period in your Gumroad balance before they become eligible for payout.

You can download a CSV report for each payout from the [Payouts dashboard](269-balance-page), which includes a breakdown of all [Gumroad fees](66-gumroads-fees) and customer data.
### Instant payouts
Creators from the US can get paid within minutes for amounts up to $10K and a 3% fee. Instant payouts are available only to creators from the US who have completed four payouts.
If you are eligible for an instant payout, you will see the option to get paid instantly on your [Payouts dashboard](https://gumroad.com/payouts).
Not all debit cards are eligible for instant payouts. If you connect a debit card and still see a message to connect a debit card to receive instant payouts, the card you connected is not eligible. You will need to try a different card.
### Enable payments via PayPal
If you have [connected your PayPal account](275-paypal-connect) with Gumroad, proceeds from all sales via PayPal will be credited to your connected account at the time of sale, in the default currency of your PayPal account. This appears on your [Payouts page](269-balance-page) as "PayPal Payouts".
### The account review process
![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5c4657ad2c7d3a66e32d763f/images/63bb9209d0b6de7e3be2a8e4/file-5aTUwIGukY.png)
For our review, we require your account to be legitimate in two major ways:
\* You comply with both [Gumroad's terms](https://gumroad.com/terms) and those of our payment processors.
\* Your account is not racking up chargebacks and suspicious purchases.
The review process can take 1-3 weeks depending on your sales and the data we can analyze. Typically, this means 3-4 sales with a balance over US$10; however, every account is unique so we thank you for your patience.
You cannot speed up the review process by purchasing your own product. Payment processors view purchasing your own products as fraudulent, and this can get you suspended or permanently banned. If you just want to test your product, please use the [test purchase feature](62-testing-a-purchase).
There can be other reasons for a payout delay that you can know more about here: [Payout delays](281-payout-delays)
