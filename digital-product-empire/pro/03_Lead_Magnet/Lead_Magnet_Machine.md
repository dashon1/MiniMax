## WHAT IS A LEAD MAGNET?

A lead magnet is a free resource you offer in exchange for someone's email address.
It's the bridge between "stranger" and "subscriber" — and subscribers are your most valuable business asset.

**The email math:**
- 1,000 subscribers × 5% conversion × $47 avg product = **$2,350 per launch**
- Launch 2x/month = **$4,700/month from email alone**
- Email list of 5,000 × 5% conversion × $97 = **$24,250 per launch**

Social followers? Borrowed. One algorithm change = invisible.
Email subscribers? Owned. Forever.

## THE 5 HIGHEST-CONVERTING LEAD MAGNET FORMATS

**Format 1: Ultimate Guide / Whitepaper (20-40 pages)**
Best for: YouTube viewers, podcast listeners, LinkedIn audiences
Conversion: 20-35%

**Format 2: Checklist / Cheatsheet (1-3 pages)**
Best for: Busy professionals who want quick wins
Conversion: 35-55% ← Highest

**Format 3: Email Course (5-10 emails over 2 weeks)**
Best for: Building deep trust before selling
Conversion: 30-45%

**Format 4: Template / Swipe File / Toolkit**
Best for: Entrepreneurs and business owners
Conversion: 25-40%

**Format 5: Video Training / Webinar Replay**
Best for: Audiences who prefer watching over reading
Conversion: 25-40%
