# MODULE 4: EMAIL MARKETING AUTOMATION
## The 'Set It Once, Profit Forever' Email System

---

## WHY EMAIL AUTOMATION BEATS MANUAL SENDING

Manual = limited by your time. You can send 20-50 emails/day manually.
Automation = unlimited. Send to 100 or 100,000 with the same effort.

**The 10-Email Welcome Sequence (auto-sent over 30 days):**

**Email 1 (Day 0):** "Your download is ready" → delivers lead magnet
**Email 2 (Day 1):** "Do this one thing today" → quick win, high value
**Email 3 (Day 3):** "Who I am and why I'm here" → personal intro
**Email 4 (Day 5):** "The thing most people get wrong about [topic]" → deep value
**Email 5 (Day 7):** "A resource that might help" → soft product mention
**Email 6 (Day 10):** "This changed everything for me" → personal transformation story
**Email 7 (Day 12):** "Quick question for you" → engagement, ask for reply
**Email 8 (Day 14):** "What [customer] said" → testimonial + soft sell
**Email 9 (Day 20):** "Something I learned this week" → high-value tip + link
**Email 10 (Day 30):** "Before you go" → final CTA with time-limited offer

---

## THE 5 EMAIL TYPES THAT GENERATE REVENUE

**1. Story Email** (builds trust) — 1x/month
"I almost gave up on this business until..."

**2. Value Bomb** (builds authority) — 2x/month
"3 things that will 3x your email open rates"

**3. Promotion Email** (generates revenue) — 1-2x/month max
"The system that generated $47K in 90 days — now available"

**4. Question Email** (builds engagement) — 1x/month
"What's the one thing holding you back right now?"

**5. Recommendation Email** (affiliate revenue) — 1x/month
"The tool I use every single day for [benefit]"
