## 📊 THE 7-FIGURE NICHE CHECKLIST

Before diving into specific niches, ask yourself these 7 questions:

**Question 1 — Pain Level**
Is this problem causing people LOSE money, time, or health? 
(The bigger the loss, the bigger the price you can charge)

**Question 2 — Budget Visibility**
Do people in this niche actively spend money to solve this problem?
If NO → skip. If YES → proceed.

**Question 3 — Evergreen vs. Trendy**
Will this problem exist in 3 years? 5 years?
(Health, money, relationships, career = evergreen. Fads = skip)

**Question 4 — Emotional Amplitude**
Do people get STRONG about this topic? 
Anger, frustration, excitement, hope?
(Weak emotions = low conversion. Strong emotions = high conversion)

**Question 5 — Audience Size**
Are at least 500,000 people actively searching for solutions?
You don't need millions — 500K is enough for a 6-figure business.

**Question 6 — Content Stamina**
Can you create 50+ pieces of content on this topic without running dry?
If you can't write 50 pieces, the niche is too narrow or too shallow.

**Question 7 — Margin Potential**
Can you deliver this product at near-zero cost (digital)?
Physical products = hard mode. Digital products = profit mode.

## 🚀 YOUR NICHE SELECTION SYSTEM

### Step 1: Pick Your Top 3 From Above (Use the Checklist)

Write down your top 3 niches from the 47 above.
Score each one 1–10 on:
- Pain level (how urgent is the problem?)
- Audience size (how many people are searching?)
- Competition (are the existing solutions bad?)
- Your expertise (do you know this topic deeply?)
- Margin potential (can you sell digital products here?)

### Step 2: Validate With 2 Hours of Research

For your top pick:
1. Google "[niche] course" — look at what's selling and for how much
2. Search Reddit "[niche]" — what are people complaining about?
3. Check Amazon — what books are bestsellers in this category?
4. Join 3 Facebook groups — what questions keep getting asked?

### Step 3: Choose Your Business Model

**Model A — Build and sell your own product**
Pros: 100% margin, full control, brand building
Cons: Takes 2–4 weeks to build
Best for: People with expertise in their niche

**Model B — PLR + Rebrand (fastest path to profit)**
Pros: Live product in 48 hours, proven content
Cons: Less brand differentiation, need to edit well
Best for: First-time sellers, testing niches quickly

**Model C — Affiliate marketing**
Pros: Zero product creation, push traffic immediately
Cons: No email list, dependent on vendor conversions
Best for: People with existing traffic or audiences

**Model D — Client services + digital products stack**
Pros: Fast revenue (land clients week 1), upsell to digital products
Cons: Requires time investment (1-on-1 or group)
Best for: People wanting $5K–$20K first month revenue

### Step 4: Set Your Launch Date

Pick a date 14 days from now.
Working backwards:
- Day 14: Launch (Gumroad/Payhip link live)
- Day 10–13: Pre-sell to warm audience
- Day 7–9: Write 3 promotional emails
- Day 1–6: Build your landing page + waitlist

## ⚠️ NICHEES TO AVOID (Red Flags)

❌ Anything requiring physical inventory (ships products, customs, returns)
❌ Anything requiring professional licenses (medical, legal, financial advice)
❌ Anything with heavy refund rates (supplements, get-rich-quick,MLM tools)
❌ Anything with declining search trends (downward Google interest)
❌ Anything where you can't explain the value in one sentence
