# MODULE 6: THE DIGITAL PRODUCT SALES FUNNEL
## The Exact Funnel System Generating $50K-$200K/Month

---

## WHY EVERY DIGITAL BUSINESS NEEDS A FUNNEL

A funnel turns cold traffic into warm buyers.
Without one, every visitor who doesn't buy = wasted money.

**The funnel math:**
1,000 visitors → 200 email subscribers → $200 tripwire + $2,820 front-end + $1,410 upsells = **$4,930/month**

---

## THE 4-STEP FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE

**STEP 1 — Tripwire ($1-$27)**
Purpose: Get first purchase, prove your market, recover ad costs
Example: "$1 trial" or "$27 PDF guide"

**STEP 2 — Front-End Product ($47-$197)**
Purpose: Your main revenue generator
Example: "$97 video course + template kit + community"

**STEP 3 — Upsell Sequence**
Purpose: Multiply AOV by 2-3x
"Add the coaching module for $97 more"

**STEP 4 — Backend Affiliate**
Purpose: Zero-cost revenue from existing customers
Promote tools/courses at 30-50% commission

---

## THE COMPLETE FUNNEL MAP

Traffic → Landing Page → Tripwire → Front-end Product → Upsell → Backend Affiliate

Recovery sequences:
- Browse abandonment: Email at 1hr, Day 3, Day 7
- Cart abandonment: Email at 1hr, Day 1, Day 3
- Recovery rate: 40-60% of lost sales
