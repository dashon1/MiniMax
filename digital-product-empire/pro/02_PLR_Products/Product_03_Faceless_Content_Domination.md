## CHAPTER 3: THE 2-HOUR VIDEO PRODUCTION SYSTEM

**The Batch Production Method:**
Create 4 videos in one sitting, schedule them for the next 4 weeks.

**Step 1 — Research (20 minutes per video)**
- Google: "[your niche] trending topics 2026"
- Find 1 core topic, write 5 variations of titles
- Collect 5 stats/facts to include in script
- Find 2 creator stories to reference

**Step 2 — Write Script (30 minutes)**
- Hook: First 30 seconds must stop scrolling
- Problem: What is your viewer suffering from?
- Solution: Your main teaching point (3–5 steps)
- Transformation: What does their life look like after?
- CTA: Subscribe, like, and hit the bell

**Step 3 — Record Voiceover (15 minutes)**
- Use ElevenLabs, CapCut, or Google Text-to-Speech
- Read the script at natural pace
- No music, no background — just clean voice for editing

**Step 4 — Find B-Roll (5 minutes)**
- Pexels.com (free, no attribution required)
- Use specific search: "person typing laptop", "money growth chart"
- Collect 8–12 clips per video (30–60 seconds of total footage)

**Step 5 — Edit Together (45–60 minutes)**
- CapCut (free): Drop voiceover → add b-roll → add text overlay → add thumbnail
- Keep cuts every 10–15 seconds (engagement rhythm)
- Add background music (Pixabay free music)
- Add captions (YouTube auto-captions + manual fix)

**Step 6 — Upload & Optimize (15 minutes)**
- Write 150–300 word description
- Add 5 tags (niche + topic + "faceless YouTube")
- Schedule for optimal upload time (2–4 PM EST is safest)

## CHAPTER 5: THUMBNAIL PSYCHOLOGY

**The 3-Second Rule:**
Your thumbnail has 3 seconds to make someone click or scroll past.

**The high-CTR thumbnail formula:**
- Big face (even if faceless, use hands/overlays to create face-like focal point)
- Bold text in 3 words maximum
- High contrast colors (yellow/black, red/white work best)
- Number or arrow to signal "there is more inside"
- Expression that shows emotion (surprise, excitement, curiosity)

**Text formula:**
"[Number] [Emotion] [Outcome]"
Examples:
"3 SHOCKING Mistakes!"
"This Tool Changes EVERYTHING"
"I Made $12K in 90 Days"

**Font rules:**
- Use Impact font (bold, readable at tiny sizes)
- White text with black outline = always visible
- Max 3 words of text on any thumbnail

## CHAPTER 6: THE SHORTS-TO-LONG STRATEGY

**Post 3 Shorts for every Long-Form video.**

**Why Shorts work for faceless channels:**
- Algorithm pushes Shorts regardless of subscriber count
- Each Short can drive 500–10K new subscribers
- Creates multiple entry points to your channel
- Content repurposing = 10x ROI on every script you write

**The Shorts workflow:**
1. Take your long-form script → extract the most controversial/dramatic 60 seconds
2. Use the same b-roll with quick cuts (every 2 seconds)
3. Add big text overlays: "This is why..." "The truth about..."
4. End with a hook to the long-form video

**The funnel:**
Shorts (60 seconds) → Channel page → Long-form video (7–15 min) → Subscriber
→ Watch time → YouTube recommends to new audiences → More subscribers
