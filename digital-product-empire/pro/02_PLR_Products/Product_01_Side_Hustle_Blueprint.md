## CHAPTER 3: FINDING YOUR MOST VALUABLE SKILL IN 30 MINUTES

Don't guess. Use this system:

**Step 1 — List your top 10 skills**
What are you genuinely good at? Not what you're "interested in" — what are you actually good at? Think: professional skills, hobbies that others ask your advice on, problems you've solved for yourself.

**Step 2 — Score each skill on 3 factors**
- Can you teach it to a beginner in under 60 minutes? (Score 1–5)
- Do people pay money to solve this problem? (Score 1–5)
- Could you create a digital product around this? (Score 1–5)

**Step 3 — Calculate your market score**
Total score = Teaching ability × Market demand × Digital scalability

Top scores = Your highest-value skill to monetize.

**The numbers:**

- 10 hrs/week = $3,000/month
- 80% profit margin (no product, just your expertise)

- Sell 50/month = $2,400/month

**Best affiliate programs for beginners:**
- Software/tools (30–50% recurring commissions)
- Online courses (20–40% one-time commissions)

- Finance products (20–50% commissions)

**The content system:**
1. Pick an affiliate product in your niche
2. Write 5 blog posts / social posts about the problem it solves
3. Put your affiliate link in every piece of content
4. Write 1 review post with your affiliate link
5. Build an email list and recommend products monthly

**The 90-day affiliate roadmap:**

## CHAPTER 12: LEGAL, TAXES, AND STRUCTURE (What You Actually Need)

**The honest truth about business structure:**
- You don't need an LLC to start
- You don't need a business bank account to start
- You don't need a lawyer to start

**What you DO need:**
1. A way to track income and expenses (Google Sheets is fine to start)
2. Set aside 25–30% of income for taxes (just save it)
3. Separate personal and business money when you hit $1K/month
4. Get a basic contract template for consulting (free templates inside this kit)

**When to level up:**
- At $5K/month: Open a business bank account (Chase Business is free)
- At $10K/month: Talk to an accountant about LLC vs. S-Corp
- At $50K/year: Hire a CPA, set up quarterly tax payments

## CHAPTER 14: SCALING TO $5K AND BEYOND

**The $5K/month milestone:**
- Requires 3 income streams
- Each generating $1,500–$2,000/month
- OR 1 flagship product at $5K/month

**The system that scales:**
1. Email list: 2,000+ subscribers at 5% conversion = $2,700/month
2. Affiliate: 3 products × 15 sales × $48 = $2,160/month
3. Service: 3 consulting clients × $500 = $1,500/month

**What stops most people at $1K/month:**
- Not enough content (need 50+ pieces before compound growth kicks in)
- No email list (social followers don't buy reliably, email does)
- Giving up too soon (most people quit at week 3, before results compound)

**The people who win:**
- Post content 3–5x/week for 90 days straight
- Build email list from week 1
- Launch a new product or affiliate offer every 30 days
- Track metrics (visitors, email subscribers, sales, revenue)
