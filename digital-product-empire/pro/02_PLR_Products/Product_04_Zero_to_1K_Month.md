## THE ORGANIC TRAFFIC PLAYBOOK

**Reddit strategy:**
Find subreddits where your target customer hangs out.
Post type: "I made $X in 30 days - here's what I did" (authentic, gets upvotes)
Post type: Answer questions with detailed, helpful responses (no spam)

**Pinterest strategy:**
Create 3 boards: "[Your Niche] Tips", "Make Money Online", "[Your Product Name]"
Post 3 pins per day for 30 days. Each pin links back to your product page.

**LinkedIn strategy:**
Post 3x per week, 5-minute posts.
Format: Story → Lesson → Question (engages readers)
