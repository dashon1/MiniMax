# The Hook System
## 10 Proven Structures That Stop the Scroll — With Examples and When to Use Each

**Rule: Write the hook first. Everything else is built to support it.**

---

## HOOK 1 — THE QUESTION HOOK

**Formula:** "Did you know...?" / "What if I told you...?" / "Have you ever...?"
**Use when:** The audience has a knowledge gap. You're promising new information.
**Best for:** Educational content, how-to threads, explanation videos.

**Examples:**
- "Did you know that 90% of people fold their arms like this? It's costing you sales."
- "What if I told you most people's morning routine is actually making them less productive?"
- "Have you ever wondered why some people get everything done and others stay busy all day?"

**Why it works:** Questions create cognitive tension. The brain needs an answer. The viewer keeps watching.

---

## HOOK 2 — THE STATEMENT HOOK

**Formula:** "The number one reason people..." / "Most people's biggest mistake is..." / "The truth about..."
**Use when:** You have a strong opinion or a universal truth to share.
**Best for:** Hot takes, controversial positions, expert authority content.

**Examples:**
- "The number one reason people fail at this is that they focus on everything except the one thing that matters."
- "Most people's biggest mistake with content is thinking more is better."
- "The truth about building an audience is that nobody tells you this part."

**Why it works:** Declarative statements create mental resistance. The viewer either agrees or disagrees — both keep them watching.

---

## HOOK 3 — THE NUMBER HOOK

**Formula:** "[Number] things you must know before..." / "The [3/5/7] step system for..."
**Use when:** The content has a clear structure and a number makes it feel achievable.
**Best for:** List content, frameworks, step-by-step tutorials.

**Examples:**
- "Five conversations you need to have before you turn 30."
- "The three-part framework that took me from zero to profitable in 6 months."
- "Seven mistakes that make people lose followers on this platform."

**Why it works:** Numbers create specificity and expectation. The viewer knows exactly what they're getting and how much.

---

## HOOK 4 — THE CONTRAST HOOK

**Formula:** "Everyone talks about X. Nobody talks about Y." / "Most people do X. The smart ones do Y."
**Use when:** You can contrast two clearly different approaches or mindsets.
**Best for:** Thought leadership, business content, personal development.

**Examples:**
- "Everyone talks about productivity hacks. Nobody talks about the identity shift that makes you actually productive."
- "Most people wait for motivation. The top performers build a system instead."
- "Everyone focuses on getting more followers. The smart ones focus on getting more comments."

**Why it works:** Contrast creates a pattern interrupt. The brain immediately processes the gap between two things.

---

## HOOK 5 — THE SCENE HOOK

**Formula:** "I just got back from a meeting where..." / "I was sitting in a coffee shop and..." / "Here's what happened to me this morning..."
**Use when:** You have a real experience that demonstrates your point.
**Best for:** Personal brands, storytelling, behind-the-scenes content.

**Examples:**
- "I just got back from a meeting where a CEO told me his biggest challenge. It took me 90 seconds to fix."
- "I was sitting in a coffee shop and overheard two founders talking about their startup. Here's what was wrong with their plan."
- "Here's what happened to me on a flight last week that completely changed how I think about time management."

**Why it works:** Real-time experience creates authenticity and immediacy. The viewer feels like they're inside your day.

---

## HOOK 6 — THE PROOF HOOK

**Formula:** "I made [amount/time] using this method..." / "I went from X to Y in [time] using..."
**Use when:** You have a specific, credible transformation to show.
**Best for:** Proof-of-concept content, results-focused messaging, business and finance.

**Examples:**
- "I made my first $1,000 in 72 hours using this exact system. Here's how it works."
- "I went from zero to 50K followers in 8 months using the same approach every single day."
- "This client increased their revenue by 40% in 6 weeks with one small change."

**Why it works:** Social proof creates credibility and aspiration. The viewer wants to know how.

---

## HOOK 7 — THE THREAT HOOK

**Formula:** "If you're doing X, stop immediately..." / "You're one decision away from..." / "The worst thing about [common habit]..."
**Use when:** You can identify something that threatens the viewer's goals.
**Best for:** Warning-style content, cautionary tales, urgent advice.

**Examples:**
- "If you're still doing this in 2025, stop. It's costing you more than you think."
- "You're one wrong hire away from losing everything you've built this year."
- "The worst thing about the productivity trap is that it feels productive."

**Why it works:** Threat hooks trigger loss aversion. The viewer needs to know if they're at risk.

---

## HOOK 8 — THE INSIDER KNOWLEDGE HOOK

**Formula:** "Here's what nobody tells you about..." / "The secret nobody talks about..." / "Nobody talks about this, but..."
**Use when:** You have knowledge that the viewer feels they should already know.
**Best for:** Expert positioning, industry secrets, professional content.

**Examples:**
- "Here's what nobody tells you about the real way to negotiate a salary."
- "The secret to networking that nobody talks about is that it's not about collecting contacts."
- "Nobody talks about this one thing, but it's the difference between a good career and a great one."

**Why it works:** Insider knowledge creates a feeling of exclusivity and discovery. The viewer feels privileged.

---

## HOOK 9 — THE RELATABLE PROBLEM HOOK

**Formula:** "You know that feeling when..." / "This is for anyone who has ever..." / "Are you one of those people who..."
**Use when:** The problem is universal enough that most viewers will feel it.
**Best for:** Lifestyle content, personal brands, emotional audience connection.

**Examples:**
- "You know that feeling when you have so much to do but you can't actually start anything?"
- "This is for anyone who has ever felt like they're falling behind in their own life."
- "Are you one of those people who plans the perfect morning but then hits snooze 6 times?"

**Why it works:** Relatability creates immediate connection. The viewer feels seen and understood.

---

## HOOK 10 — THE "DID YOU KNOW" STAT HOOK

**Formula:** "[Stat/proven fact] — and it changes everything about how you think about [topic]."
**Use when:** You have a specific, surprising, credible statistic or research finding.
**Best for:** Educational content, scientific findings, data-driven content.

**Examples:**
- "90% of startups fail not because of the product — but because of this one thing that nobody addresses."
- "Harvard research found that the most successful people do this one thing differently. Here's what it is."
- "Neuroscience says your first thought in the morning shapes your entire day. Here's what most people get wrong."

**Why it works:** Authority from data creates instant credibility. The viewer trusts the information.

---

## HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT HOOK

| Your Content Type | Best Hooks to Start With |
|---|---|
| Educational / How-to | Question, Number, "Did You Know" stat |
| Business / Thought leadership | Contrast, Statement, Insider knowledge |
| Personal / Lifestyle | Scene, Relatable problem, Question |
| Results / Proof | Proof, Number, Statement |
| Warning / Caution | Threat, Question, Statement |

**Rule:** Write 3 different hooks for the same video. Pick the strongest. Film all 3. Test which one performs best.
