# AI Dialogue Generator
## Prompts to Generate Full Video Scripts from a Topic in 90 Seconds

**How to use:** Pick the prompt that matches your format. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude with your topic details. Get a complete, production-ready script in under 2 minutes.

---

## PROMPT 1 — THE 60-SECOND SHORT-FORM SCRIPT

**Use when:** You need a TikTok, Reel, or YouTube Short script, fast.
**Time to generate:** 60–90 seconds

```
Write a 60-second video script for a [platform] video.

My topic: [Your topic]
Target audience: [Who it's for]
The one main idea: [The single point to communicate]

Requirements:
- The script must start with a hook in the first 3 seconds
- The hook must immediately create curiosity or a pattern interrupt
- The body must cover [specific angle or sub-point]
- End with a specific CTA (not "let me know in the comments")
- Include B-roll suggestions in brackets [like this] for every cut point
- Use natural spoken language, not written language
- Include a surprising stat or specific detail to add credibility
- Total script should be approximately 150 words

Format:
HOOK: [3 seconds — face to camera, the one line that stops scrolling]
BODY: [~50 seconds — the main content with B-roll cues in brackets]
CTA: [~7 seconds — specific action, not generic]

Write the script now:
```

## PROMPT 2 — THE 90-SECOND CONTENT PIECE

**Use when:** You need a slightly longer short-form video.
**Time to generate:** 90 seconds

```
Write a 90-second video script for [platform].

Topic: [Your topic]
Audience: [Who it's for]
Desired outcome: [What the viewer should feel or do after watching]

Requirements:
- Open with a pattern interrupt hook — no "hey guys" or "today I want to talk about"
- The first sentence must create curiosity, not introduce yourself
- Include exactly [3/4/5] distinct points
- Each point must be supported by [a specific example / a statistic / a story]
- End with a clear reason to follow or save, not a generic CTA
- Include [B-roll cues in brackets] for visual cut points
- Write in spoken language — as if you're talking to a friend, not presenting
- Include one conversational transition phrase like "Here's the thing" or "The real point is this"
- Do not use the words "in this video" or "subscribe and hit the bell"

Format:
HOOK (0:00–0:05):
[Face to camera, the one sentence that makes them stop scrolling]

BODY (0:05–0:83):
[Main content with B-roll cues]

CTA (0:83–0:90):
[Clear ask with a reason]

Write the full script now:
```

## PROMPT 3 — THE YOUTUBE EXPLAINER SCRIPT

**Use when:** You want a complete YouTube video with intro, body, and outro.
**Time to generate:** 2 minutes

```
Write a complete YouTube video script.

Video title: [Your title]
Video length: [Target length, e.g., 8–10 minutes]
Target audience: [Who it's for]
The transformation: [What the viewer will know or do after watching]

Structure:
1. HOOK (first 30 seconds): Open with a pattern interrupt. No "hey what's up guys." Lead with a question, a stat, a scene, or a bold statement that promises a specific outcome.
2. PROBLEM AGITATION (1–2 minutes): Establish the problem your viewer is facing. Make it specific. Make it feel urgent.
3. AGENDA (30 seconds): "In this video I'm going to cover [X, Y, Z]" — tell them exactly what they're going to learn.
4. CONTENT (main body): Break into [3–5] sections. Each section should be 2–3 minutes max.
5. CLOSING: Summarize the key points. Tell them the one thing to do next.
6. CTA: Ask for a specific action (not just "like and subscribe" — be specific about why they should and what they'll get)

Style:
- Conversational, not formal
- Use "you" and "I" not "one" and "people"
- Include B-roll cues in brackets [B-roll: description] at every major point
- Include verbal calls to action: "if you're still here, drop a comment"
- Include pattern interrupts every 2–3 minutes (a visual cut, a tone shift, a surprise element)

Script now:
```

## PROMPT 5 — THE PERSONAL STORY SCRIPTER

**Use when:** You want to turn a personal experience into a video script.
**Time to generate:** 60 seconds

```
Write a 60-second personal story script from this experience:

What happened: [The event]
When it happened: [Timeframe]
Who was involved: [People]
The turning point: [What changed]
The result: [What happened after]
The lesson: [What you learned]

Requirements:
- Hook: Start mid-scene, not with "I want to tell you about" — start as if you're already in the moment
- Structure: Follow the classic story arc — Situation, Complication, Turning Point, Resolution
- Emotion: Let the story feel real — include at least one moment of vulnerability or tension
- B-roll cues in brackets [like this] at key visual moments
- Spoken language — write it as if you're telling a friend at a coffee shop
- The lesson should feel discovered, not taught — avoid "the moral of the story is" language

Write the script now:
```
