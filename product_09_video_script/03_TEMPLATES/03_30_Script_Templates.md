# 30 Script Templates
## Ready-to-Film Scripts Organized by Type and Platform

**How to use:** Pick a template that fits your topic. Fill in the [brackets]. Film. Post.

Each template has:
- Hook (first 3 seconds)
- Body (middle 30–50 seconds)
- CTA (final 5–10 seconds)

## EDUCATIONAL SCRIPTS

---

### SCRIPT 1 — THE HOW-TO EXPLAINER
**Best for:** YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Topic fit:** Teaching a skill, explaining a process

```
HOOK (0:00–0:03):
"The fastest way to [skill] is to stop doing [old approach] and start doing this instead."

BODY (0:03–0:53):
"Here's the [number]-step version that most people skip.

Step 1: [Action]. Here's why it matters: [2-sentence explanation].

Step 2: [Action]. The mistake most people make is [common error]. What you do instead is [correct approach].

Step 3: [Action]. This is the one most people skip because [why]. Don't skip it — [outcome if you do it].

That's it. Three steps. That's the entire system."

CTA (0:53–0:60):
"If you want the full breakdown, save this and follow along — I break this down completely in my [caption/content]."
```
