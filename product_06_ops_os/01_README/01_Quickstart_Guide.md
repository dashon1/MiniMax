## The Core Idea: Your Business Has 5 Inputs

Every solo business runs on the same 5 operational inputs. Each one, handled manually, costs you time. Each one, automated, runs while you sleep:

| Input | What It Is | What Happens When You Automate It |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Capture** | New people finding you | They get added to your CRM, tagged, and enter your pipeline automatically |
| **Client Delivery** | Work you deliver | Project tracker updates, client notified, next steps created automatically |
| **Finance Tracking** | Money in and out | Income categorized, invoice logged, profit tracked without spreadsheets |
| **Content Creation** | Content you publish | Published content triggers repurposing, social scheduling, and archive |
| **Communication** | Messages and replies | Routine replies automated, urgent messages tagged and surfaced |

The 5 automations in this kit cover all 5 inputs.

---

## The 5 Automations

**Automation 1 — Lead to Pipeline**
Form submitted → CRM populated + welcome email sent + task created to follow up

**Automation 2 — Client Project Tracker**
New project → Notion project created + team notified + kickoff calendar event + first invoice drafted

**Automation 3 — Finance Auto-Log**
Payment received → Income logged to spreadsheet + tagged by type + monthly summary triggered

**Automation 4 — Content-to-Archive**
New content published → Logged to content calendar + social cross-post triggered + resource library updated

**Automation 5 — Auto-Response Engine**
Inbound message → Categorized (lead / client / press / spam) → correct response template sent → flagged if urgent

After 2–3 hours, your entire business runs on an operating system. You will have one dashboard to check instead of 12 open tabs.
