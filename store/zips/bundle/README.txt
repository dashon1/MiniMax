## The Universal Workflow (All Kits)

1. Open the script → customize to your voice
2. Record voiceover OR use AI voice (ElevenLabs recommended)
3. Combine voiceover + b-roll video in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (both free)
4. Upload thumbnail to YouTube Studio
5. Copy-paste the SEO package (title, description, tags)
6. Post the Short clip as a teaser BEFORE the main video
7. Pin the comment template from the SEO file immediately after publishing
