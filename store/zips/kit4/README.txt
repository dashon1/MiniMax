## Quick Start (5 Steps)

1. **Personalize the script** — swap "Dashon" for your name, adjust the case study to fit your story
2. **Record voiceover** OR use AI voice (confident male/female voice recommended)
3. **Upload your thumbnail** — swap the AI face for YOUR photo if you want a personal brand
4. **Paste the SEO package** — includes "Comment BRAND" engagement mechanic
5. **Post the Short** — the contrarian hook is highly shareable

## Pro Tip on the Thumbnail
The AI thumbnail works great to start. But for a personal brand channel, your REAL face on the thumbnail will ALWAYS outperform a stock/AI face long-term. When you're ready, take a photo with the same pose and expression and swap it in.
