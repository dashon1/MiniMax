**MOVE 2: Inflation-Hedge Your Savings**

[SHOW CHART: Inflation rate vs. standard savings account APY over 5 years]

If your savings are sitting in a regular bank savings account right now earning 0.01% — which many major banks still offer — you are actively losing purchasing power every single day. Not hypothetically. Actually.

[PAUSE]

Here are three specific tools to inflation-hedge your savings:

**I-Bonds.** These are U.S. Treasury-issued savings bonds whose interest rate is tied directly to the inflation rate. You can buy up to **$10,000 per person per year** directly through TreasuryDirect.gov. When inflation is high, your I-Bond rate is high. It's one of the cleanest inflation hedges available to everyday investors.

**TIPS ETFs.** If you want broader exposure, Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities — TIPS — are bonds where the principal adjusts with inflation. A convenient way to access them is through an ETF like **SCHP** from Schwab. Low cost, liquid, and directly tied to inflation metrics.

**High-Yield Savings Accounts.** This one's simpler. Many online banks are still offering 4–5% APY on high-yield savings accounts. That's not exciting, but it's meaningfully better than watching inflation eat your emergency fund alive.

[TEXT OVERLAY: "I-Bonds | TIPS ETFs (SCHP) | High-Yield Savings — Protect What You Have"]
