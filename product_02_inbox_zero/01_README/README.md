## HOW IT WORKS — THE 4-SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

**System 1 — Auto-Triage**
Every email lands in your inbox → Zapier detects it → GPT reads it → GPT assigns a priority label → You see at a glance what's urgent vs. what's routine.

**System 2 — Auto-Draft**
Important emails get a GPT-generated reply draft created in your drafts folder. You open, review, edit if needed, and hit send. Takes 30 seconds instead of 5 minutes.

**System 3 — Escalation Routing**
Emails from certain senders (key clients, your boss, investors) automatically get starred, labeled "urgent," and moved to a priority inbox section. No more important emails buried under newsletters.

**System 4 — Daily Digest**
At 8am and 5pm, GPT compiles a briefing: "Here's what you have today, what's most important, and what can wait until tomorrow." You read one document instead of scanning 80.
