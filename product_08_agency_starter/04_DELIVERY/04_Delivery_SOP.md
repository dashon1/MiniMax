# Delivery SOP
## The Complete System for Delivering Your Agency Service — On Time, On Scope, Without Chaos

**Time to build: 4 hours | Notion + Google Docs (free) is sufficient**

---

## THE 4-STAGE DELIVERY FRAMEWORK

Every client engagement follows the same 4-stage system. Once you build it once, you run it for every client automatically.

```
STAGE 1           STAGE 2           STAGE 3           STAGE 4
ONBOARDING   →    EXECUTION    →    DELIVERY    →    REVIEW
(Sets up)         (Does the work)    (Hands over)      (Confirms)
```

---

## STAGE 1 — ONBOARDING (Days 1–2)

**Goal:** Get everything you need to start the work, and make the client feel confident.

### Day 1: Contract Signed + Payment Confirmed

**Automatic actions (set these up once, run forever):**
1. Contract signed → Notion client page created
2. Payment confirmed → Welcome email sent automatically
3. Welcome email includes: What happens next, when you'll be in touch, what they need to send you

**Your Day 1 checklist:**
- [ ] Confirm payment received
- [ ] Add client to Notion (name, email, company, service, start date, price)
- [ ] Send welcome email (use template below)
- [ ] Add kickoff call to calendar (within 2 business days of signing)
- [ ] Create project folder in Google Drive

**Welcome Email Template:**

```
Subject: You're in — here's how we start

Hi [Name],

Great to have you on board. I'm [Your Name] and I'll be your [service type] contact.

Here's what happens next:

1. Kickoff call — I've sent a calendar invite for [date/time]. We'll use this to confirm the full scope and answer any questions.

2. What I need from you — Before our call, if you could share [2–3 specific assets or info], that would be helpful. Usually: [list what you need, e.g., brand guidelines, existing content, access to account, etc.]

3. What to expect — I'll send you a quick check-in every [weekly/different cadence] with a status update. You don't need to chase me.

Looking forward to getting started.

[Your Name]
```

### Day 2: Kickoff Call (30 minutes)

**Agenda:**
1. Confirm scope (5 min) — "Let me make sure I understand the main deliverable. Here's what I'm planning to deliver: [summary]. Does that sound right?"
2. Timeline (5 min) — Confirm delivery date based on scope
3. Communication (5 min) — How often should you check in? What channel? Who is the point of contact?
4. Access and assets (10 min) — What do you need from them? Set a deadline for them to provide it.
5. Questions (5 min) — Any concerns or clarifications from their side?

**After the call:**
- Send meeting summary email (within 2 hours)
- Update Notion client page with agreed scope and timeline
- Add project milestones to Notion
- Set first milestone reminder

## STAGE 2 — EXECUTION (Days 2–Delivery)

**Goal:** Do the actual work. Communicate progress. Never surprise the client.

### Weekly Status Update (Every Week on the Same Day)

Send this every week regardless of whether there's a big update:

```
Subject: Week [X] — [Project Name] Update

Hi [Name],

Quick update on where we are:

✓ Completed this week:
- [What you finished]
- [What you finished]

→ In progress:
- [What you're working on right now]

Next this week:
- [What's coming up]
- [Next milestone]

Any blockers or anything I need from you: [specific ask]

The project is on track for delivery on [date].

[Your Name]
```

**Rule:** If you missed something or a deadline is at risk, tell the client before the weekly update — not after.

### Managing Scope Creep

When a client asks for something outside the agreed scope:

1. **Acknowledge** — "Yes, that's a good point."
2. **Name the impact** — "That addition would add about [X hours / X days] to the timeline. It would also mean adjusting the delivery date."
3. **Present options** — "We have two choices: either we adjust the scope to make room, or we add this as an additional phase at [additional price]."
4. **Decide and document** — Record the agreed change in Notion. Send a follow-up email confirming.

**Never say yes to out-of-scope work without adjusting timeline or price.**

## STAGE 3 — DELIVERY (Delivery Day)

**Goal:** Hand over work in a way that feels complete, professional, and easy to accept.

### 48 Hours Before Delivery

Send this message:

```
Subject: [Project Name] — delivery tomorrow

Hi [Name],

Just a heads up: I'll be sending over the completed [deliverable] tomorrow by [time].

It will include:
- [What's included in the delivery]
- [Any documentation or guide]
- [What to expect on the handover call]

Nothing you need to do today — just wanted you to know it's coming.

[Your Name]
```

### The Delivery Email

On delivery day, send:

```
Subject: [Project Name] — delivery

Hi [Name],

It's ready. Here's everything you need.

DELIVERABLE:
[Link to file/folder] — [description]

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- [Included item 1]
- [Included item 2]
- [Included item 3]

REVISION NOTES:
- [Any notes about the work, decisions made, things to be aware of]

NEXT STEPS:
- [What to do with this deliverable]
- [If applicable: what happens next in terms of support/access]

I'm available for a 30-minute call this week if you'd like to walk through anything together.

If you have any questions or need revisions, reply to this email.

[Your Name]
```

## STAGE 4 — REVIEW (Days 1–7 After Delivery)

**Goal:** Confirm satisfaction, close the project officially, and set up the next engagement.

### Day 3 After Delivery

```
Subject: How does it look?

Hi [Name],

I hope you've had a chance to take a look at the [deliverable]. How is it sitting with you?

Any questions or anything that needs adjusting — I'm here.

Also, if you're happy with it, I'd love to hear what you're going to do with it next. Always great to see it in action.

[Your Name]
```

### Day 7 After Delivery — Final Check-In

```
Subject: 7-day check-in — [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

It's been a week since delivery. Just wanted to check in:

1. Is everything working as expected?
2. Any questions that came up since?
3. Are there any follow-up deliverables we discussed that we should schedule?

If everything is good, I'll close the project file and we'll be done with this phase. I'll send over a final summary.

[Your Name]
```

### Closing the Project

When client confirms satisfaction, send:

```
Subject: Project closed — [Project Name]

Hi [Name],

It's been a pleasure working with you on this. The project is now officially closed.

For your records: here's a summary of what was delivered.
[1-line summary of deliverable]

If you need anything else in the future, you're welcome to reach out.

Best of luck with everything.

[Your Name]
```

### Exit Interview Question (Ask for a Review)

Before closing, ask:

> "Would you be open to sharing a brief testimonial or review about your experience working together? If you're happy with the outcome, I'd love to capture what worked in your words."
