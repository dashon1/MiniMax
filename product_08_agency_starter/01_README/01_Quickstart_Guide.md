## What You Are About to Build

A complete, launch-ready service offer that you can have live within 24 hours. Not a business plan. Not a website. An actual, sellable service offer with a delivery system, a sales script, and a client workflow — built and ready to go.

This kit gives you:
- **Offer Templates** — Pre-built service packages that clients can actually buy
- **Sales Script Prompts** — AI prompts that help you sell without feeling salesy
- **Delivery SOP** — The exact step-by-step for delivering the service
- **Client Workflow System** — How to manage clients from first contact to final delivery

---

## The 24-Hour Launch Plan

**Hour 1 — Choose Your Offer**
Pick from 3 pre-built offer templates based on what you already know how to do. Each template has a defined scope, deliverable, and price point.

**Hours 2–4 — Customize Your Offer**
Fill in the offer template with your specific skills, timeline, and pricing. The structure is done — you just add your details.

**Hours 5–8 — Build the Delivery System**
Follow the Delivery SOP to document exactly how you will deliver the service. This becomes your working playbook.

**Hours 9–12 — Write the Sales Script**
Use the Sales Script Prompts to generate a natural, low-pressure conversation guide for selling your offer.

**Hours 13–18 — Set Up the Client Workflow**
Use the Client Workflow System to set up your CRM, onboarding steps, and communication templates.

**Hours 19–24 — Soft Launch**
Send your offer to 10–20 people in your network. Get feedback. Make one or two adjustments. Start getting clients.

## Who This Is For

This kit works if you:
- Have a skill you can sell as a service (writing, design, strategy, automation, etc.)
- Have been planning to launch but keep getting stuck in the research loop
- Want a structured path from "I have an idea" to "I have a paying client"

This kit does not require:
- A website
- A business name
- A logo
- Any formal business registration

Start with what you have. Add the rest later.
