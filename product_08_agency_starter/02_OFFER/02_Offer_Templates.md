# Offer Templates
## 3 Ready-to-Launch Service Packages — Complete with Pricing, Scope, and Pitch Scripts

---

## HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

Each template is a complete service package. To launch with it:

1. **Choose** the offer that closest matches your skill
2. **Customize** the scope, timeline, and pricing for your situation
3. **Copy** the pitch script and send it to 10–20 people
4. **Launch** and refine based on feedback

Do not overthink this. The offer template is the hard part — you are customizing it, not creating from scratch.

## OFFER 1 — THE "DO-IT-FOR-ME" PACKAGE

**Best for:** Writers, designers, developers, consultants, strategists
**Type:** One-time project delivery
**Price range:** $297–$997

### The Offer

> "I'll [specific deliverable] for you in [timeframe], for [price]."

**Example:**
> "I'll build your AI automation workflow system in Notion — 5 custom automations, documented and ready to run — for $497."

### What's Included (The Package)

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **The Deliverable** | Exact scope of what the client receives |
| **Timeline** | When they get it (e.g., "delivery in 5 business days") |
| **Revisions** | How many revision rounds are included (e.g., "2 rounds included") |
| **Communication** | How you'll communicate during the project |
| **Support** | What happens after delivery (e.g., "7 days of support included") |

### Scope Definition Template

```
SCOPE: [Specific thing you do]
FOR: [Type of client who needs this]
DELIVERABLE: [Exactly what they receive]
FORMAT: [How it's delivered — document, file, call, etc.]
TIMELINE: [How long it takes you to deliver]
REVISIONS: [Number of revision rounds]
SUPPORT: [Post-delivery support period]
PRICE: [Your price]
```

### Pitch Script

```
Subject: [One sentence problem + your offer]

Hi [Name],

I help [type of client] who are dealing with [problem].

Most people in your position have tried [what they usually try], but it usually [the limitation].

I've just started offering a [service name] that does [the outcome] — specifically designed for [their situation].

Here's what it includes:
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

The investment is [price] and the timeline is [timeframe].

If that sounds useful, I'd be happy to book a 20-minute call to see if it's a fit.

[Your name]
```

## OFFER 2 — THE " DONE-FOR-YOU + TRAINING" PACKAGE

**Best for:** Coaches, consultants, specialists, agency owners
**Type:** Project delivery + knowledge transfer
**Price range:** $997–$2,497

### The Offer

> "I'll [specific deliverable], and I'll train your team to run it themselves."

**Example:**
> "I'll build your content system in Notion — automated, documented, and fully trained to your team — for $1,497."

### What's Included (The Package)

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **The Deliverable** | The completed system or asset |
| **Training** | How you'll teach them to use it (video walkthrough, documentation, live walkthrough call) |
| **Documentation** | Written SOPs they can reference forever |
| **Handover Call** | A live session where you walk them through the complete system |
| **Support** | Post-handover support period (typically 14–30 days) |

### Scope Definition Template

```
SCOPE: [System or asset you build]
FOR: [Type of client]
DELIVERABLE: [What you build]
TRAINING INCLUDED: [Format — video, docs, live call]
DOCUMENTATION: [What gets documented]
HANDOVER CALL: [Duration — e.g., 60-minute live walkthrough]
SUPPORT PERIOD: [How long you're available after handover]
PRICE: [Your price]
```

### Pitch Script

```
Subject: A complete [system name] — built and handed over to your team

Hi [Name],

If you've been thinking about [outcome], but keep running into the problem of [specific issue], I have something that might be exactly right.

I just opened a [service name] package for [type of client]. Here's what it includes:

1. [Deliverable] — built specifically for your situation
2. [Documentation] — step-by-step instructions your team can follow forever
3. [Training] — I'll walk your team through the entire system live
4. [Support] — [X] days of follow-up support included

The investment is [price] and the timeline is [timeframe] from signing.

I only have [number] spots available this month. If you'd like to see if it's a fit, reply and I'll send you a quick questionnaire.

[Your name]
```

## OFFER 3 — THE "MONTHLY RETAINER" STARTER

**Best for:** Strategists, VA-style services, ongoing content, automation maintenance
**Type:** Recurring monthly engagement
**Price range:** $497–$1,997/month

### The Offer

> "I'll handle [ongoing task] for you every month — at [price] per month."

**Example:**
> "I'll manage your content system and publish 8 posts per month across all platforms — for $597/month."

### What's Included (The Package)

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Monthly Deliverable** | What you produce each month (e.g., 8 posts, 5 automated workflows, 4 blog posts) |
| **Communication Cadence** | How often you check in (e.g., weekly call, daily Slack) |
| **Reporting** | What you deliver each month (e.g., monthly report with metrics) |
| **Response Time** | How quickly you turn things around |
| **Contract Length** | Monthly rolling or minimum 3-month commitment |

### Scope Definition Template

```
SERVICE: [What you do each month]
FOR: [Type of client]
MONTHLY DELIVERABLE: [Exactly what they get each month]
PLATFORMS/SCOPE: [Where/how you deliver]
COMMUNICATION: [Check-in frequency and channel]
REPORTING: [What you deliver monthly]
RESPONSE TIME: [Turnaround speed]
CONTRACT: [Monthly rolling / minimum 3 months]
PRICE PER MONTH: [Your monthly rate]
```

### Pitch Script

```
Subject: Ongoing [service] — handled for you at [price]/month

Hi [Name],

I want to let you know about something I'm offering for [type of client] who want [ongoing outcome].

Instead of [what they'd usually do], I handle it for them — consistently, every month.

Here's what's included:
- [Monthly deliverable 1]
- [Monthly deliverable 2]
- [Monthly check-in/report]

It's [price] per month, month-to-month, no long-term commitment required. You can cancel any time.

This works best for people who [ideal client description].

If that sounds useful, I'd love to schedule a quick call to see if we're a good fit.

[Your name]
```

## PRICING YOUR OFFERS

Use this framework to set your price:

| Offer Type | Factors | Suggested Range |
|---|---|---|
| One-time project | Time required, complexity, your experience | $297–$997 |
| Done-for-you + Training | Deliverable value, training time, documentation | $997–$2,497 |
| Monthly Retainer | Ongoing scope, hours/month, exclusivity | $497–$1,997/month |

**Do not price based on time.** Price based on the value of the outcome you create.

A $497 automation system that saves someone 5 hours a week is worth $497+ if the outcome is clear and the client earns more than that in time saved.
