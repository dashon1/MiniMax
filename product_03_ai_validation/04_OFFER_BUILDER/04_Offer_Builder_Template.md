## Section 1: The Problem Statement (AI-Generated Draft)

**Answer these 3 questions to build your problem statement:**

1. What is the ONE specific problem your buyer is facing?
   *(Be as specific as possible: not "time management" but "constantly missing deadlines because they have no system for triage" — not "productivity" but "spending 3 hours on email instead of doing actual work")*

2. What is the COST of this problem (time, money, emotions)?
   *Example: "Loses 2 hours per day = 10 hours/week = 500 hours/year. At $50/hr opportunity cost = $25,000/year in lost earning power."*

3. What does your buyer currently tried that ISN'T working?
   *(The wrong solutions make your offer the obvious next step)*

---

**AI DRAFT — Problem Statement:**
*Fill the above, then use this frame to generate:*

"[BUYER PERSONA] who is trying to [SPECIFIC GOAL] keeps getting derailed by [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]. Every time they try to fix it, they end up [WHAT'S GOING WRONG]. Meanwhile, the cost is [REAL COST]. They need a way to [DESIRED OUTCOME] without [MAIN PAIN].

They've tried [EXISTING ATTEMPTS] but those don't work because [WHY THEY FAIL]. What they actually need is [YOUR SOLUTION CATEGORY] — specifically one that [THE KEY THING YOUR PRODUCT DOES]."
