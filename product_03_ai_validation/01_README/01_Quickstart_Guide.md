## What You Are About to Build

Most people spend months building a product nobody wants. They guess, assume, and hope. Then they wonder why it doesn't sell.

This kit gives you the opposite workflow. In 60 minutes, you'll know whether your idea has real demand, what to charge for it, and how to write the landing page that converts — with AI doing 80% of the intellectual heavy lifting.

This is not theory. This is a production system with 5 interlocking components:

1. **Validation Checklist** — the gate before you build anything
2. **AI Prompt Scripts** — run these to extract real demand signals from real data
3. **Offer Builder Template** — turns your validated idea into a pricing tier and package
4. **Landing Page Copy Generator** — output-ready headlines, bullets, and CTA copy
5. **Decision Engine** — the Yes/No gate that tells you whether to proceed or pivot

## The 60-Minute Workflow

### Phase 1: Validate (20 minutes)

**Step 1.1 — Run the Validation Checklist (File: 02_CHECKLIST)**
Open the checklist. Answer every question honestly. Score your idea 1-10 on each criterion. The checklist is designed to surface the 3 most common killer mistakes: fake demand, wrong audience, and weak framing.

**Step 1.2 — Run the Demand Testing Prompts (File: 03_PROMPTS)**
Open ChatGPT (or Claude). Paste Prompt A1 (Reddit demand scrape). Paste Prompt A2 (Google autocomplete validator). Paste Prompt A3 ( competitor absence check). Save the outputs. These are your demand receipts — not guesses.

**Step 1.3 — Score Your Results**
Use the Scoring Guide at the end of the checklist:
- Score 70+: Strong signal — proceed to Phase 2
- Score 40-69: Conditional Proceed — fix the weak points, rerun prompts
- Score below 40: Pivot or deep-dive needed — do not pass Go

---

### Phase 2: Build Your Offer (20 minutes)

**Step 2.1 — Fill the Offer Builder (File: 04_OFFER_BUILDER)**
Open the template. The Offer Builder forces you to answer 8 questions that determine pricing and positioning. AI generates a draft from your answers. You refine. The output is a complete one-pager with your product name, promise, target buyer, tier structure, and pricing.

**Step 2.2 — Set Your Pricing**
Use the pricing framework in the Offer Builder:
- **Impulse tier**: $9–$19 (for checklists, one-page templates, short guides)
- **Core tier**: $27–$47 (for SOPs, frameworks, templates with 5+ files)
- **Premium tier**: $67–$97 (for courses, done-with-you, deep systems)
- **Signature tier**: $197+ (for high-automation, white-glove, or consulting-adjacent)

---

### Phase 3: Write Your Landing Page (20 minutes)

**Step 3.1 — Use the Copy Generator (File: 05_LANDING_PAGE)**
Feed your completed Offer Builder into the Landing Page Copy Generator. AI produces all the sections you need:
- Hook headline (5 variations)
- Subheadline / value restatement
- Problem section (amplified)
- Solution section (your offer)
- Features list (specific and benefit-led)
- Testimonial slots (swap in real ones after launch)
- Pricing block (with tier framing)
- Guarantee block
- Final CTA sequence

**Step 3.2 — Assemble on Carrd, Gumroad, or Payhip**
Use the copy output to populate a simple one-page landing. Link to your Gumroad or Payhip product. Publish.

## Quick Reference: Common Mistakes

| Mistake | What Happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Building before validating | 6 months wasted on a product nobody wants | Run Phase 1 first, always |
| Charging too little | Undermines perceived value, attracts bad customers | Follow the pricing tiers above |
| Vague problem statement | Copy doesn't convert | Use the Problem Reframer in Prompts file |
| No competition | Either no market or too early | Run Prompt A3 before anything |
| No social proof slots | Landing page feels untrustworthy | Add "What early buyers say" section even with placeholder text |

## What to Do If You Get Stuck

**AI gives generic output:**
Your inputs are too vague. Go back and fill the Offer Builder with more specifics. AI reflects the quality of your inputs — "email course" is a much better prompt than "digital product."

**Your idea scores low:**
This is good news, not bad news. You found out in 20 minutes instead of 6 months. Iterate on the idea or try a adjacent niche using the same prompts.

**You're unsure about pricing:**
Look at what similar products charge. Add one tier above what feels comfortable — you'll often find the higher tier converts better due to anchoring.
