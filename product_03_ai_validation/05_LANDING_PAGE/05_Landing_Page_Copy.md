## MASTER AI COPY GENERATION PROMPT

*After filling in your product details in the Offer Builder, paste this single prompt into ChatGPT to generate all 13 sections at once:*

---

**Prompt:**

"Generate a complete landing page copy for my digital product using the details below. Write in a direct, confident tone — no fluff, no marketing clichés, no generic phrases like 'game-changer' or 'transform your life.' Write like a smart friend explaining something useful.

**Product Name:** [YOUR PRODUCT NAME]
**Product Tagline:** [ONE-LINE TAGLINE]
**Primary Audience:** [SPECIFIC BUYER TYPE AND NICHE]
**Core Problem:** [WHAT PROBLEM YOUR PRODUCT SOLVES]
**Core Outcome:** [WHAT THEY GET AFTER USING IT]
**Time to Result:** [TIMEFRAME]
**Product Format:** [WHAT'S INSIDE — NUMBER OF FILES/DELIVERABLES]
**Tiers and Pricing:**
- Tier 1: [NAME] at $[PRICE] — [WHAT'S INCLUDED BRIEFLY]
- Tier 2: [NAME] at $[PRICE] — [WHAT'S INCLUDED BRIEFLY]
- Tier 3: [NAME] at $[PRICE] — [WHAT'S INCLUDED BRIEFLY]

Generate all 13 sections:
1. Hero headline (5 variations, each under 10 words)
2. Subheadline (one sentence, restates the value)
3. Problem section (4-part amplification: false belief, real cost, spiral, emotional close)
4. Solution section (bridge from problem to product — your product as obvious answer)
5. Who this is for (4 buyer traits that fit) and who it's NOT for (3 disqualifiers)
6. Features as benefits (4 bullets using the format: "The [X] gives you [Y] — so you can [Z]")
7. Social proof section (write 2 pre-launch placeholder testimonials using real-sounding personas)
8. Pricing block with tier framing (one-line framing per tier)
9. Guarantee block (pick the most appropriate option from: corruption, satisfaction, results)
10. Primary CTA (button copy) and secondary CTA (text link copy)
11. FAQ (answer these 6 questions in 1-3 sentences each)
12. Final CTA section (headline + button copy + post-click expectation)
13. Footer one-liner (the product in one sentence)

Format each section with a header and the copy clearly labeled."
