# Product Idea Validation Checklist
## The 20-Question Gate Before You Build Anything

**Score each question 1–5:**
- 1 = Not true at all / No evidence
- 3 = Partially true / Some evidence
- 5 = Completely true / Strong evidence

Add your scores. See the Scoring Guide at the bottom.

## Section 2: Offer Viability (40 points max)

**Q9. Can you solve this problem with information or a system — not just a feeling?**
Digital products work best when the solution is a process, framework, checklist, or template.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q10. Is the solution something that can be PACKAGED and DELIVERED instantly?**
No phone calls, no live sessions, no custom consulting. If it can be a PDF/video course ZIP — score 5.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q11. Can you create the product WITHOUT needing a professional credential?**
Life experience + research is enough. You don't need a PhD or 10 years in the industry.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q12. Can you create the product in 2 weeks or less working part-time?**
Products that take longer than 2 weeks often never get finished. Be honest.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q13. Can you explain the outcome in ONE sentence?**
"If you [do X], you'll [get Y]." If you can't, the offer is too vague to convert.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q14. Is there a clear buyer persona who would buy this TODAY?**
Not "anyone who wants to get better at X" — a specific role, situation, and urgency.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q15. Do you have a rough idea of your pricing?**
Follows the tier structure: Impulse $9–$19 | Core $27–$47 | Premium $67–$97 | Signature $197+
Score: ___ / 5

## Section 3: Conversion Feasibility (20 points max)

**Q16. Is the outcome measurable or observable?**
"Can they show before/after?" is a strong conversion signal. Vague improvements are hard to sell.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q17. Does this fit an existing purchase pattern?**
People are used to buying things like this — courses, templates, toolkits. Score lower if you're introducing a genuinely new category to your audience.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q18. Do you have a credible angle or personal story that makes you the RIGHT person to sell this?**
"I learned this the hard way so you don't have to" is a real angle. Generic authority is lower.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q19. Can you write 3 bullet points that make a non-buyer say "I need this"?**
If you can't visualize strong bullets right now, the marketing hasn't caught up to the product.
Score: ___ / 5

**Q20. Is there a channel where your buyer ALREADY HANGES OUT CONTENT about this problem?**
Organic reach + existing community = faster sales. Starting from zero is possible but harder.

## Scoring Guide

Add all 20 scores: ___ / 100

| Score | Signal | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **75–100** | STRONG green light | Proceed to Offer Builder immediately |
| **55–74** | CONDITIONAL PROCEED | Address the 3 lowest-scoring questions before moving on |
| **35–54** | REVISIT OR PIVOT | The idea has legs — find the weak spot. Test an adjacent angle. |
| **0–34** | PAUSE AND REDESIGN | Significant gaps exist. Consider pivoting or repositioning before building. |

---

## Minimum Viable Scores by Section

You need strong numbers in each section to succeed:

- **Section 1 (Demand):** 28/40 minimum — if people aren't already paying for this, it's a hobby not a business
- **Section 2 (Offer):** 28/40 minimum — a bad offer kills a good idea faster than no demand
- **Section 3 (Conversion):** 14/20 minimum — even with demand and a good product, bad marketing = no sales

**If you score below the minimum in any section, go back and improve that dimension before spending time building.**
